# Honour Health Brand Style

## Overview

Honour Health is Dr Victoria Ball's acupuncture and Chinese medicine clinic at Buddina on the Sunshine Coast, with Dr Erin Horne as the second practitioner. The name carries weight for Vicki, who spent a year working in Chinese hospitals and holds the word honour in high regard - the clinic is a place where patients stop, slow down, rest and honour themselves. The positioning is evidence-informed Traditional Chinese Medicine, warm when talking about how a patient will feel and clinical when talking about treatment.

The visual character is calm coastal premium. Dawn light over water plus cherry blossom, carried by photography rather than by loud colour. The ground stays light (white and a warm shell paper), the palette works as accents, and one deep maroon is reserved for the thing you want clicked.

Two constraints shape everything and are not negotiable. First, the brand runs under Australian health practitioner advertising law (AHPRA, National Law s133) - no testimonials, reviews, ratings, before and after imagery, packages or voucher promotions anywhere, so trust is built from registration, memberships, years and research. Second, the clinic's audience is mostly women aged 35 to 60 reading on a phone, often in the evening, looking for help with menopause, fertility, stress or pain. Every decision is judged on that phone first.

Australian English throughout. This is a Pro Marketing client.

**Color scheme:** light only. The navy footer and bands are structural devices, not a dark theme.

**Accent rule:** maroon means act. If everything is maroon, nothing is. Navy carries headings and links where a second dark is needed so maroon stays the one action colour. Ocean is the large-type and UI accent and never carries body copy.

**Balance:** roughly 60 percent white and shell paper, 30 percent tints and slate structure, 10 percent maroon, navy and ocean. Section rhythm alternates white, shell, white, ice. Blush is not a fourth ground - it is the warm moment.

**Contrast, computed:** maroon 11.8 on white, 10.9 on shell, 9.8 on blush, 10.1 on ice. Navy 12.3, 11.3, 10.3, 10.5. Slate 6.2, 5.7, 5.2, 5.3. Ocean 4.2 on white and 3.5 to 3.8 on tints, which is why it is large text and UI only. White on any tint is 1.1 to 1.6 and fails - never place white text on a tint.

## Typography

**Rufina** (400 and 700, self-hosted WOFF2 from Google Fonts) is the heading serif, chosen live with the client as "still professional but with a little bit of extra". Display and headings run at 700. Title Case for display names.

**Open Sans** (variable, 400 to 700, self-hosted WOFF2) is the body and interface face. It won over Sintony, Montserrat, Comfortaa and Roboto in the 17 July meeting; those four are rejected on record and are not to be re-litigated. Weights are capped at 700.

| `{typography.button}` | Open Sans | 15.7px | 600 | 1.2 | Buttons and pills |

### Principles

- **The eyebrow is a documented style.** `{typography.label-md}` above a heading is part of the brand guide. Generic design linters ban an uppercase label above every heading; on this brand the ban is a standing, deliberate override. Frequency is still a judgement - one eyebrow per section, not one per card.
- **Numerals are always Open Sans, never Rufina.** Rufina's 0 and 1 read as O and I, so "01" read as "OI" in three of the five concepts before the numerals moved to the sans. This holds for chapter numbers, card numbers, counters and prices.
- **Serif for the idea, sans for the interface.** Rufina carries headings and pull lines. Open Sans carries everything a visitor reads at length or operates.
- **Body measure is 65ch.** Inner pages use a 65ch reading column. Never edge-to-edge text on any width.
- **Weights 600 to 700, never 800.** House taste. Display is Rufina 700, UI labels are Open Sans 600 or 700, nothing heavier.

## Layout

8px base rhythm. 1200px content wrap (`{spacing.wrap}`), gutter `{spacing.gutter}` on every wrap element at every width.

Section rhythm alternates white, shell, white, ice, with blush reserved for the warm moments (women's health, the final CTA). Full-bleed photography bands are allowed between sections; text never is.

Inner pages (service and article) use a three-column layout on desktop - a sticky page index on the left, the 65ch reading column, and a sticky booking rail on the right - collapsing to a single column with a chip-row index below 900px. The rail exists because a two-column version left a dead right rail at 1440.

The homepage follows a fixed nine-section blueprint from the design plan - hero, trust bar, four modality cards, five condition cards plus a chip row to the other seven, the two practitioners as 2x3 rounded portraits, the evidence strip, first visit, find us with a map, and the final CTA on blush.

## Elevation & Depth

Photography carries the depth. Hairlines (`{colors.outline-variant}`) do the structural work. Shadows are soft, warm and rare - a card hover lift at most, never a shadow on everything.

Real photography over generated heroes. The clinic shoot (21 February 2025, about 100 frames) is catalogued with a consent ledger; identifiable patient faces never ship uncleared. Team portraits shoot 2x3 portrait with rounded corners on the web. Coastal Point Cartwright imagery is a known gap in the shoot and would be generated if a coastal direction wins.

The blossom motif is modernised as one large, very low opacity line-art branch used as a section watermark (maroon or pink line, 3 to 6 percent opacity), structural and quiet, never the clip-art clusters of the old site.

## Shapes

`{rounded.default}` 10px on buttons, inputs and small cards, `{rounded.lg}` 18px on photo frames and large cards, as set in the shared tokens. The five concepts deliberately vary this (6px ruled grid in E, 24px soft frames in C, arches and pills in D), so the radius is UNLOCKED until the client picks a direction. Whatever wins becomes the locked value here.

Photographs are always rounded on the web. Whole logo always visible, never cropped.

## Components

### button-primary

Maroon fill, white label, Open Sans 600, 10px radius, 14px by 24px padding. At most one filled maroon action per viewport. Hover deepens to `{colors.primary-hover}`. Label is "Book Online Now" (Cliniko) wherever the action is a booking.

### button-secondary

Navy fill, white label, same shape, hover deepens to `{colors.secondary-hover}`. Used for the second action where two are needed, or as the rail card action on inner pages.

### link

Maroon, underlined - never colour alone. Citation links (`a.cite`) are smaller, dotted-underlined and do not wrap.

### card

White on a tint, `{rounded.lg}`, 24px padding, a `{colors.outline-variant}` hairline or a soft shadow depending on the concept. Cards for modalities and conditions carry a photo or numeral, a Rufina heading, one corpus sentence trimmed at a sentence boundary, and a "Learn more" or "Read more" link. Never a testimonial card.

### trust-bar

The AHPRA-compliant substitute for social proof - registration (CMR numbers), AACMA membership, Sunshine Coast since 2003, over 30 years in complementary medicine. The umbrella figure and the clinic-anchored figure never share a sentence.

### booking-rail

Sticky card on inner pages - heading, one line on the 60 minute initial consultation and HICAPS, the primary action, the phone number, the hours table.

### faq

Native `details` and `summary`, one open at a time is not required, FAQPage schema in the head. The merged FAQ lives on the service page, not on standalone pages.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Judge every page on a 375 to 430px phone first, then desktop. Measure the gutter, do not eyeball it - the padding gate (`concepts/scripts/edgecheck.mjs`) exists because body text once shipped flush to the glass.
- Keep one maroon action per viewport and repeat it, do not reinvent it.
- Carry "Doctor of Traditional Chinese Medicine" on the first "Dr" mention on every page, and the Safety Sentence wherever treatment is discussed. Templates bake both in.
- Use `padding-top` and `padding-bottom` on any element that also carries the wrap class. The `padding: X 0 Y` shorthand silently deletes the gutter.
- Set numerals in Open Sans.
- Write to house style - no em or en dashes, straight quotes, no colons in headings, Australian English.
- Keep motion inside the timing bands (see Motion). Calm means low amplitude, not slow.

### Don't

- No testimonials, reviews, star ratings, review embeds, before and after imagery, packages or vouchers. Ever. This is law, not taste.
- No white text on any tint.
- No ocean blue as body text.
- No Rufina numerals.
- No weights above 700.
- No second action colour. Emphasis that is not maroon comes from weight, size or a change of surface.
- No content hidden until a script reveals it in a way that a visitor perceives as the page being slow. See Motion.
- Never `scrollreveal`.
- No cure, fix, eliminate or guaranteed language, and no superiority claims over other professions - the voice rules live in the content repo's regen-rules and override everything at generation time.

## Icons

No icon set is specified in the brand guide. The concepts use inline SVG for the scroll cue and menu glyphs only. The house icon system for Pro Marketing builds is Font Awesome 7 Pro; adopt it here only when the production build needs icons, and record the decision in this section when made.

## Motion

GSAP is the house engine, effects briefed by number and name from The Motion Index (pmw-motion-index.pages.dev). Each concept carries one signature treatment on top of the shared fade-up default. Reduced motion and the `#static` hash render everything settled, and the pre-hide class is only added by script, so a page with no JavaScript is fully visible.

Timing bands, from the fork's motion governor, are the rule here -

| Duration | Use |
|---|---|
| 100 to 150ms | Immediate feedback - hover colour, press |
| 150 to 300ms | Routine state change, card lift, underline draw |
| 300 to 500ms | Layout shift or overlay opening, the mobile menu |
| 500 to 800ms | One authored entrance per page, and only one |

Exits run faster than entrances. Arrivals use `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Transform and opacity only.

Standing finding (4 September 2026) - the concept study's shared reveal runs 1.1s with a 0.12s stagger per child, hero entrances 0.9s with staggered delays, and signature effects to 1.4s, on every section. Measured page speed is excellent (Lighthouse 100 mobile, LCP 1.6s) but the site is perceived as slower than a site with no reveals at all, because content arrives late by design. Bring every duration inside the bands and reveal one or two elements per view, not every card.

## Responsive Behavior

Breakpoints in use are 1200 (rail drops), 900 (three columns to one, nav collapses to the sheet menu), 600 and 520 (card grids to one column). Test at 360, 375, 390, 414, 430 and 768 as a minimum, and never ship with sideways scroll - decorative bleeds (glows, watermarks) are clipped at the page, not at the element.

Touch targets hold 44px in the nav and buttons; the WCAG 2.2 floor for other controls is 24px with 8px between neighbours.
